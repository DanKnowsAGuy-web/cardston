The plan

Three steps. Nothing invented, everything measured.

  1. Share your data

    A signed authorization for a year of interval electricity data, plus recent utility bills. No site visit required to start.

  2. Get your plan

    We build a real, measured plan from Cardston's own numbers, not a sales estimate. The town sees the numbers before anything is signed.

  3. We handle funding and install

    We work the funding options on Cardston's behalf, arrange the financing, and manage the install. The town's job is to say yes.

Funding & financing

Programs we'll work to get you into, wherever you qualify.

We check every program below for Cardston. Nothing here is a guarantee or an amount; it's the list of doors we knock on, and their status as of September 2026. Full detail and sources in the sources section.

ProgramLevelStatusNote
FCM Green Municipal Fund, Community Buildings Retrofit Federal, delivered by FCM Open Rolling intake; all Canadian municipalities eligible except a short excluded list
NRCan / Housing, Infrastructure and Communities Canada, Green and Inclusive Community Buildings Federal Closed Current intake closed; program runs to 2029, we watch for the next round
Canada Infrastructure Bank, Building Retrofits Initiative Federal Open, via lenders Delivered through financial partners; best fit for a larger pooled project
Canada Community-Building Fund (now Building Communities Strong Fund, Community Stream) Federal, flows through Alberta Town's own allocation Cardston already receives this yearly; council can choose to direct part of it here
Alberta Local Government Fiscal Framework Alberta Town's own allocation Formula funding Cardston already receives; usable on infrastructure priorities within program rules
MCCAC programs (Community Energy Conservation, Municipal Electricity Generation) Alberta Winding down MCCAC is being wound down province wide; existing signed agreements proceed, new intakes are limited
Emissions Reduction Alberta Alberta Not a fit today Current calls focus on industrial and technology projects, not small municipal buildings; we monitor for new calls
Cardston's own municipal electric utility Local To confirm Cardston runs its own distribution utility rather than FortisAlberta; we'll check directly for any utility side program

Towns don't pay income tax, so federal tax credits generally don't apply to Cardston directly. Where a private financing partner sits inside the structure, a credit like the federal Clean Technology Investment Tax Credit could apply to that partner and show up as better pricing to the town, never as a credit Cardston claims itself.
